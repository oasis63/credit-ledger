# Credit Ledger privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026. Replace the bracketed parts before publishing, and have it reviewed for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023._

Credit Ledger helps distributors record credit given to retail shops, payments received, bills and salesman visits. It is operated by **[your name or company], [address]** ("we").

**Data we process**
- **Distributor staff:** name, email address and phone number, used to sign in and to show who recorded each entry.
- **Retail shops (customers of the distributor):** shop name, owner name, phone number, address, GSTIN, and credit and payment history. The distributor enters this data and controls it. We process it on the distributor's behalf.
- **Location:** captured only when a salesman checks in at a shop, and only while the app is open. It is used to pin the shop on the route map. The app never tracks location in the background.
- **Device data:** entries made offline are stored on the phone until they sync, and the sign-in token is kept in the phone's secure storage.

**How it is used**
- To run the ledger, bills, reminders and reports for the distributor.
- To send WhatsApp payment reminders and receipts to shops when the distributor asks for them, through Meta's WhatsApp Business API.

**What we don't do**
- No ads, no selling of data, and no sharing except with the service providers needed to run the service: hosting in India, and WhatsApp messaging.

**Storage and security**
- Data is stored on servers in India.
- It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Each distributor's data is isolated from the others at the database level (row-level security).

**Retention and deletion**
- Data is kept while the distributor's account is active.
- A distributor can ask us to delete their account and data.
- A shop owner can ask their distributor, or contact us, to correct or delete their details. Records the law requires to be kept, such as GST invoices, are retained for the legally required period.
